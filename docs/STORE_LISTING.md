## 3. Long Description (2,250 Characters)

```text
Never lose proof of a payment again. Slip Vault is your 100% offline personal payment slip ledger and screenshot organizer.

Do you have hundreds of transfer confirmation screenshots from JazzCash, Easypaisa, SadaPay, NayaPay, Raast, or your bank buried in your photo gallery? When a customer, vendor, or landlord asks, "Did you send the payment on the 12th?", finding that one slip can take minutes of painful scrolling.

Slip Vault solves this in seconds.

⚡ INSTANT ON-DEVICE OCR & AUTO-EXTRACTION
Import any payment screenshot or share it directly from your gallery into Slip Vault. Our on-device text engine reads the slip completely offline and automatically extracts:
• Transaction Amount (in PKR)
• Payment Direction (Money In / Money Out)
• Sender / Receiver Name
• Masked Mobile & Account Numbers
• Bank or Wallet Provider (JazzCash, Easypaisa, SadaPay, NayaPay, Raast, Meezan, Alfalah, HBL, UBL, and more)
• Transaction ID (TID / Ref No) and Date

🔍 SEARCH THE TEXT INSIDE ANY SCREENSHOT
Slip Vault features an ultra-fast SQLite FTS5 search engine. Search by counterparty name, transaction ID, bank name, or any text printed on the image. You can even find Wi-Fi passwords, order receipts, and utility bills in under 100 milliseconds.

📊 AUTOMATIC MONTHLY CASH FLOW & REPORTS
See your monthly financial summary at a glance with Net Cash Flow, Total Money Received, and Total Sent. Export formatted monthly PDF statement summaries and Excel-compatible UTF-8 CSV spreadsheets with a single tap.

💳 CLEAN WHATSAPP RECEIPT CARDS
Stop sending cluttered full-screen screenshots showing your phone's battery percentage and personal notifications. Slip Vault generates beautiful, professional payment confirmation cards ready to share directly to WhatsApp.

🔒 100% PRIVATE & OFFLINE BY DESIGN
Your financial life is nobody else's business:
• Zero cloud servers. No external accounts or logins required.
• Zero internet connectivity needed for OCR or storage.
• Biometric App Lock: Protect your records with fingerprint or face recognition.
• Local Encrypted Backups: Export and restore full ZIP archives locally.
• Urdu language support and dark mode built-in.
```

## 7. Play Console Questionnaire Answers

### 7.1 Content Rating (IARC)
* **Category:** Utility / Productivity / Personal Finance
* **Violence, Sexual Content, Controlled Substances:** No to all.
* **User Interaction:** Users do not communicate directly with other users via app servers.
* **Result:** **Rated for 3+ / Everyone**.

### 7.2 Target Audience and Content
* **Target Age:** 18 and older.
* **Appeal to Children:** Not designed for children.

### 7.3 Ads Declaration
* **Does your app contain ads?** **Yes** (Google AdMob SDK integrated in free tier).

### 7.4 Data Safety Form Answers
* **Does the app collect or share user data?** **Yes** (Only what Google AdMob collects).
* **User Financial Information (Bank details, transactions, names, amounts):** **NOT COLLECTED**. User content stays 100% local on device.
* **Data collected by AdMob:**
  * Device or other IDs (Advertising ID) — for Advertising & Analytics.
  * Diagnostics (Crash logs, performance metrics) — for App functionality.
* **Is data encrypted in transit?** Yes (all AdMob & Google Play network traffic uses TLS/HTTPS).
* **Can users request data deletion?** Yes (all data is deleted locally by uninstalling or using in-app delete).

### 7.5 Financial Features Declaration
* **Category:** Personal Financial Management (PFM) / Manual Ledger.
* **Declaration:** "The app is a manual on-device ledger and receipt organizer. It does not provide banking, loans, credit, wire transfers, or regulated payment services."
